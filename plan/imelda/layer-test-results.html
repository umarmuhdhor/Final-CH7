Where the numbers come from

  • L2: ai/test-layer2/REPORT.md (25 scam) and REPORT-legit.md (23 legit). Average = mean of the per-URL score column.
  • L3: ai/test-layer3/HASIL-L3-PENUH.md (25 scam + 10 legit, 29 Oct 2026). "Fields + text" = the field + teks column. Time from section 4.
  • L4: ai/test-layer4/HASIL.md (25 scam + 10 legit + 1 trap, 108 calls, 30 Oct 2026). Average points = flags × 30 ÷ pages, from the +30 rule in reality.js.
  • Live run: ai/test-layer-live/v1/HASIL.md. Old vs new: ai/test-layer-update-full/v1-2026-11-02/HASIL.md.

Read with care

  • Times are measured on a Mac over 127.0.0.1, not from a phone and not from Cloudflare. Real latency will differ.
  • L3's slow time is RDAP. At the 1.5 s limit the age signal is dropped, so the "today" scores move between runs (15–18 of 25 scam ≥ 30).
  • Settings differ per test: L3 ran with L4 off, L4 ran with the production gate skipped, the live run had L1 off. The layers were not measured in one single run.
  • Only 25 scam pages, and several share one kit (for example 12, 13, 16). Do not read 14.1 or 36.1 as a rate for real traffic.
